## Telegram bots and Mini Apps
Bots, shops and games inside Telegram. Payments via TON Wallet, Telegram Wallet, Crypto Pay and MetaMask, plus NFTs and Telegram Gifts. Stack: Bot API, Mini Apps, TON, Crypto Pay. He has also built bots for the MAX messenger.

## AI and LLMs in your product
Assistants, automatic request handling, document parsing, RAG and agents. Models are wired in so people actually use them, not just for the demo.

## Web apps and SaaS
Admin panels, dashboards, marketplaces and customer portals on Laravel with Vue/Nuxt, or on Next.js, with Stripe and PayPal payments.

## Backends under load
Queues, Kafka, Redis, PostgreSQL and MongoDB tuning. He took the heaviest reports from 10 seconds to under one.

## Scrapers and automation
Puppeteer data collection, pipelines doing 50k records a day, media and backups in S3.

## Deploy and support
Docker, CI/CD on GitHub Actions and GitLab CI, Nginx, Linux. Several releases a week without manual fuss.
