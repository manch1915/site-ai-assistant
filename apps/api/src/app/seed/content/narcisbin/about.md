# About Levon Oganesian

Levon Oganesian is a full-stack developer based in Yerevan, Armenia, working remotely. He positions himself as "Full-stack · AI · Telegram". He has six years of commercial development behind him, since 2020. His core stack is PHP/Laravel with Vue/Nuxt. For the last two years he has mostly built Telegram bots and Mini Apps with crypto payments, LLM integrations and backends built on queues and Kafka.

He usually owns a project end to end: database schema, backend, frontend, deploy and support. He is also happy to take a single piece of a project.

Numbers he can point to: 15+ freelance projects taken from the first call to launch; about 1 million events a day through a Kafka pipeline; API p95 under 200 ms in production at Yavvi.ai; about 40% less manual work for operators after LLMs were added at Yavvi.ai; heavy reports sped up from 10 seconds to under one second; about 100k monthly active users on ArmadaBoost and about 50k users on the NFT Mini Apps.

Languages: Armenian (native), Russian (C1), English (B2).
