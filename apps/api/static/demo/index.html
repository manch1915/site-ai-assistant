How a repair goes

No appointment needed. Booking a slot online only guarantees a technician is free.

  1. Diagnostics

    About 20 minutes at the counter. Free if you go ahead with the repair, otherwise €15 for phones and €25 for laptops.

  2. Fixed quote

    You get the price and the time before we start. If we find something else inside, we call you first.

  3. Repair

    Screens and batteries take about an hour. Laptops take one to three working days. Parts we have to order arrive in two to five days.

  4. Pay on pickup

    Card, MB Way or cash. You only pay once you have checked the device works.
